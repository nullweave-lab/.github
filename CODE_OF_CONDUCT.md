# Code of conduct

Nullweave Research is committed to a technical community in which participants can examine claims, challenge assumptions, report failures, and disagree without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss evidence, methods, and consequences rather than attacking people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observation from inference.
- Respect responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Do not publish personal information, credentials, or identifying material without explicit authorization.
- Do not fabricate results, citations, affiliations, or reproduced behavior.
- Give appropriate credit for prior work and reported vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate deanonymization, doxxing, credential exposure, plagiarism, falsified evidence, and publication of unresolved harmful exploitation details are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project. Reports about conduct should use a private repository contact path where available. Public reports should contain only enough information to request private contact.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented internally, and limited to project participation.
